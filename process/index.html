The editorial standard

Research the person. Then listen to what they say.

Preparation is not a script the host has to follow. It is context: the key moments, the question worth asking, and the room to follow a more interesting answer.

  • Specific questions, not generic prompts
  • Useful context before the guest arrives
  • A flexible conversation structure
  • An edit that keeps the human moments
