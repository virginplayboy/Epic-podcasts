About a fund podcast.

Can you help us decide what the show is about?

Yes. We can work with your team to clarify the audience, recurring themes and format before the episode work begins.

Can guests come from our portfolio?

Yes. We can source and research guests from your portfolio, your network and the wider ecosystem around the topics you want to cover.

How much time does our team need to spend?

Your team brings the expertise and records the conversation. We take on the guest research, preparation and editing around it.
