What changed

Give each idea something to show.

The work stayed inside the existing format and focused on how each video looked, sounded and moved.

01 / CAPTIONS

Make text land

Reworked the style and motion so captions added emphasis rather than only transcription.

02 / VISUALS

Show the idea

Added curated animation and B-roll to help the audience see what was being described.

03 / SOUND

Shape the feeling

Added sound design to give the edit an emotional shape and more energy.

The lesson

Sometimes the biggest change is helping a good idea feel good on screen.
