Questions founders ask

Before the first episode.

Do I need a fully formed show concept?

No. We can start with what you want to talk about, who you want to reach and what kind of conversations you enjoy having.

Will the script make the conversation feel stiff?

The research and question flow are there to give the host confidence and context. They create a route through the topic, not a word-for-word performance.

Can you work with our existing podcast?

Yes. We can join an existing show and help with the guest pipeline, research, episode preparation and editing.
