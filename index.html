A few useful answers

Before we press record.

What does end-to-end podcast production include?

Guest sourcing, guest research, episode research and scripting, pre-production planning, editing and post-production. We shape the work around your show.

Do you work with podcasts that already exist?

Yes. Epic Podcast is for venture capital firms and startup founders who already have a podcast or are ready to build one.

Can you help us find guests?

Yes. Guest sourcing is part of the offer, alongside research that helps each guest become a genuinely interesting conversation.

Will you help shape the questions and episode?

Yes. We research the guest, develop the episode angle and prepare the question flow with the host’s voice and goals in mind.
